The license for the EarPT app is Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Standard EULA), unless App Store Connect says otherwise.

In short: the app is licensed, not sold. You may use it on devices tied to your Apple ID, under Apple’s rules. We are responsible for the app’s content; Apple runs the store.

2. Purchase

  • You get ten workout sessions to try the app, at whatever floor placement set. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks every floor and track as they exist at the time of purchase.
  • Payment and refunds are handled by Apple. Ask Apple Support first for IAP refunds.
  • This is a license to use the gym, not a promise of new rooms forever, and not a subscription. We may stop selling the app or stop shipping updates. The copy on your device should keep working as long as the system allows.
  • We do not promise lifetime support or that the app will remain in the App Store.

EU/EEA: withdrawal rights for digital App Store purchases follow Apple’s consumer terms.
