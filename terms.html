1. The app — EULA

The license for the EarPT app is Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Standard EULA), unless App Store Connect says otherwise.

In short: the app is licensed, not sold. You may use it on devices tied to your Apple ID, under Apple’s rules. We are responsible for the app’s content; Apple runs the store.

2. Subscription

  • Payment, renewal, and cancellation are handled by Apple. Manage them in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or in the App Store.
  • Price and length are shown in the app before you confirm (yearly with a trial, or monthly).
  • The trial becomes a paid yearly subscription unless you cancel in time, under Apple’s terms.
  • Floor 1 in the app stays free. Higher floors and other tracks need an active subscription or an open trial.

EU/EEA: withdrawal rights for digital App Store purchases follow Apple’s consumer terms. Ask Apple Support first for IAP refunds.
