Why ear training

Hear it. Then you can play it.

Ear training is learning to recognize pitches, intervals, chords, and rhythm by ear. It closes the gap between what you hear in your head and what you play or sing — so you learn songs faster, catch mistakes as they happen, and improvise without guessing.

Mind to hands

What you hear inside gets a name. Then you can put it on the instrument, without hunting.

Songs land faster

A line you hear is a line you can take. Less transcribing in the dark. More tunes in your hands.

Freedom to improvise

When the color is clear you don't have to guess. You can leave the scale — and find your way back.

A trained ear is musical freedom.
