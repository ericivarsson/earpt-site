Why ear training

Hear it. Then you can play it.

Ear training is learning to recognize pitches, intervals, chords, and rhythm by ear. It closes the gap between what you hear in your head and what you play or sing — so you learn songs faster, catch mistakes as they happen, and improvise without guessing.

Mind to hands

What you hear inside gets a name. Then you can put it on the instrument, without hunting.

Songs land faster

A line you hear is a line you can take. Less transcribing in the dark. More tunes in your hands.

Freedom to improvise

When the color is clear you don't have to guess. You can leave the scale — and find your way back.

A trained ear is musical freedom.

FAQ

Ear training, short.

What is ear training?

Ear training is learning to recognize pitches, intervals, chords, and rhythm by ear. It closes the gap between what you hear in your head and what you play or sing.

How does EarPT work?

Open the app. Do a session or two. A short level check sets the floor. You stay in the room until it's clean. Five minutes, most days.

Piano or guitar?

A sampled grand piano and a mic'd acoustic guitar. Organic color — wood and string, not a toy synth. Switch in Settings.

Is it on iPhone?

EarPT is an iOS app. It's on its way to the App Store.

Is it a subscription?

No. Ten workouts are free. Then a one-time unlock. Not a subscription.

How do you train to hear chords?

Listen to a voicing, name the quality, do it again tomorrow. EarPT keeps you in the room until it's clean — major, minor, sevenths, then more color.

How do you train to hear melody?

A short phrase. You hear it, then you place it. Five minutes, most days. The line sticks because you come back.

How do you train to hear tones and intervals?

Two notes, in key. You name the distance. Simple when that's the job. Wider and faster when the ear is ready.
