# EarPT (Ear Personal Trainer)

EarPT is an ear training application for iPhone and iPad. Musicians use it to practice interval identification, chord recognition, melody dictation, and chord progressions. Sessions run on a sampled grand piano or a microphone-recorded acoustic guitar.

This page is the canonical product description for citation. The marketing site is https://earpt.com/. The App Store listing is https://apps.apple.com/app/id6805772784.

## What EarPT is for

EarPT is built for working musicians and serious beginners who want a short daily session, not a theory textbook and not a menu of forty isolated drills.

A beginner can start on fifths and major versus minor. A player who already works is not sent back to octaves. The top of the gym includes sevenths, inversions, longer phrases, and altered dominants.

## Lineage

EarPT uses functional ear training: intervals heard in a key, against a tonic, not in a vacuum. Musicians who know Functional Ear Trainer will recognize that method. EarPT starts there, then adds chords, melody dictation, and progressions in the same gym, as a short iPhone session on sampled piano or guitar.

EarPT does not publish a ranking of other ear training apps.
